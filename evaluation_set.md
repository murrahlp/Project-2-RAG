## 1. What weapons are prohibited on campus?
   
**Answer:** Prohibited weapons include but are not limited to: firearms (including those carried under a concealed
carry permit or license), pellet or BB guns, ammunition, knives (other than small utility tools), explosives,
fireworks, chemicals intended to cause harm, laser pointers, swords, axes, paintball guns, and any object
used or intended to be used in a threatening or dangerous manner.

## 4. What is the lock out policy on campus ?
   
**Answer:** Residents are expected to carry room and building keys (and IDs) with them at all
times. Locked doors and attention to keys are essential to students’ personal safety and the safety of
their property. In the event a student is locked out of their room during a college work day, the student
should call the Residence Life Office (608-363-2350). If it is after office hours or on the weekend, the
student should contact Security, who can let them in. If the student loses their key, they should contact
Residence Life immediately, fill out a key replacement form, and take it to Security to have a new key cut
for their room.

## 6. What is the academic honesty policy on campus ?
    
**Answer:** In an academic institution, few offenses against the community are as serious as academic
dishonesty. Such behavior is a direct attack upon the concept of learning and inquiry and casts doubts upon all measures of achievement. Beloit insists that only those who are
committed to principles of honest scholarship may study at the College.

## 9. What are the four princples of Restorative Justice ?

**Answer:** 1. Inclusive decision-making in the hands of the people most closely involved in the incident.
2. Accountability that makes people who cause harm take responsibility and make amends.
3. Reparation and healing that uplifts harmed parties (not drag down offenders).
4. Rebuilding relationships focusing on trust and mutual wellbeing.
