
# AI Use Log
## Ingest
Chatgpt was used to help with reading the pdf's of the course catalog and student handbook, results were verified by manually looking at a handful of txt documents, the manifest, and document count matching the csv
## Pipeline
Google AI was used to assist with the pipeline, specifically the chunking and embedding sections, this was verified through counting the chunks and making sure it gave a reasonable count, as well as counting the amount of distinct vectors. 
## Evaluation Metrics
Gemini was used to help code and check the evualation metric. This took many tries and failures using different prompts and checking if it was actually counting the questions answers correctly.
