1. Emergency Notifications & Community Alerts (Timely Warnings)

The Safety and Security Office is the primary office which sends “Rave Alerts,” also known as “Emergency
Notifications,” via the Rave Alert mass notification system. We utilize email, text, and phone calls to alert
our campus about an active and immediate threat to the life safety of campus. The College uses the
CLEAR framework when determining whether an Emergency Notification should be issued.

● C – Confirmed
● L - Life-threatening
● E - Emergency
● A - Active (or Imminent)
● R - Reportable Geography (on our campus or public property directly adjacent to it) at large.

The Safety and Security Director, Emergency Operations Center Manager, Dean of Students, Human
Resources Director, and other Senior Leadership may be involved with the decision to send a Rave Alert if
there is ample time to consider whether the threat is truly CLEAR. We will only send out an “ALL-CLEAR”
alert if we already sent a Rave Alert, and only once it is reasonably safe to resume normal activities.

The College also issues Community Alerts, also known as Timely Warnings, through email
communications using the [Safety] tag. These notifications are intended to inform the College community
about Clery-reportable crimes or other safety concerns that represent an ongoing or continuing threat
within the College’s Clery Geography, even when the threat is not considered an immediate or imminent
danger to life.

Not all crimes or hazardous incidents will result in a Timely Warning. Timely Warnings are issued when
they are determined to be appropriate and likely to assist in protecting the campus community or
preventing similar incidents. Decisions regarding whether to issue a Timely Warning are made on a
case-by-case basis and typically involve the Director of Safety and Security in consultation with
appropriate College officials, such as the Dean of Students or Human Resources Director.

2. Guests of Students

Any guest(s) of a Beloit College student must have a guest pass while on the College campus or
attending College events. Guest passes are available from the Campus Security Office on the lower level
of Pearsons Hall. College administration and the Security Office reserve the right to refuse or revoke a

guest pass without prior notice. Violators of the Guest Pass Policy are subject to prosecution for
trespassing. Please be sure to follow the guidelines listed below.
1. Only Security can issue Guest Passes.
2. Both the sponsor and their guest must come to Security together. They both must show
photo IDs. A maximum of four (4) guests per student is allowed. No exceptions.
3. Guests are expected to stay with their host. (Noting the three-day limit for guests.)
4. Hosts are responsible for the conduct and behavior or their guest(s).
5. The sponsoring student must personally know their guest(s) and will be responsible for their
guest’s conduct, including adherence to student regulations and consideration for the rights of
others.
6. Guests must have their passes with them at all times and are required to present them on
request.
7. Passes are valid for a period of three days. If further visitation is desired, Security may renew the
pass for a maximum of three extra days. Any extension beyond those three extra days must be
approved by the Office of Residence Life after consultation with any roommates, if applicable.
8. Guest passes ARE NOT required for families of enrolled students during a daytime visit. Guest
passes ARE required for families of enrolled students if they stay overnight on campus.
9. Graduates of Beloit College may be issued a guest pass upon request without a sponsor.
10. Guest passes will not be issued to anyone under the age of 18, unless they are a younger sibling of
an enrolled student.
11. Former students who withdrew in good disciplinary standing may be issued a guest pass with
a sponsor.
12. If there is an admission fee to an event for non-students, the guest will be expected to pay such a
fee.
13. Guests must park on city streets, and must observe alternate side parking. Guests may park in
campus parking lots with permission from security.

Entertainment passes-Coughy Haus/Bands/Entertainment
Frequently bands and hired professional entertainers will request entertainment passes for family,
friends, crew and record label visitors. Students are not allowed to sponsor these “guests of the band.”
The number of “visitors” each entertainer is allowed will be specified at the time of the contract. All
contracts must be reviewed, processed, and approved by staff in the Office of Student Engagement and
Leadership. The entertainer’s visitors will be issued an entertainment pass to the Coughy Haus or place
of venue which allows them access only to the location of their performance, not campus parties, etc.
Since students do not know these individuals, it is a violation of the campus guest policy to list them as
your personal guests. In addition, off-campus attendees, unless known by the student personally,
cannot be sponsored by a student. Violation of either of these policies is subject to disciplinary action
and the full range of sanctions (see Judicial/Hearing System). Entertainers may not publicize their
performance(s) in newspapers, press releases, the internet Web, etc. to avoid problems with unexpected
and uninvited off-campus audiences.

Can townspeople or high school students come in to hear a great band in C-Haus?

No, to both. A student (and current faculty and staff members) can always invite guests, but any
non-student attending an event or party, even outside of the Coughy Haus, needs a guest pass issued by
the Security Office, and signed by the student taking responsibility for hosting that individual. High
school students are minors and cannot be in a facility with the sale of alcohol, ever.

3. ID (Identification)
All students are required to possess a valid Beloit College identification card and carry it with them
at all times on campus. IDs are for the student’s security and convenience on campus, and are
often required for admission to events and for the use of many facilities. They are not transferable.
Students who loan their ID to access food service are in violation of this policy. Students are
required to show their IDs promptly at the request of any College faculty or staff member or
security officer. The ID card is your permit card and should not be altered or tampered with in any
fashion. Misuse and/or tampering/altering of an ID card may subject a student to disciplinary
action. Again, this policy is for the protection of each student’s safety. Students who lose their ID
card should report the loss to the Residence Life Office on the 1st floor of Porter Hall; a
replacement ID card/key card will be made and the student will be charged.. The ID card is the
property of Beloit College.

4. Missing Resident Policy
If a member of the College community has reason to believe that a student is missing, contact Security
at (608) 363-2355 immediately.

In compliance with the Higher Education Opportunity Act (2008) Missing Student Notification Policy and
Procedures, it is the policy of Beloit College to actively investigate any report made to Security about a
missing resident who is enrolled at the College and resides in on-campus housing.* Each student will be
notified of the Missing Resident Policy and Procedures via this Student Handbook.

A student may be a “missing person” if the person’s absence is contrary to their usual pattern of
behavior, or unusual circumstances may have caused the absence. Such circumstances could
include, but not be limited to, a report or suspicion that the missing student is in a life-threatening
situation, has been with persons who may endanger the student’s welfare, has trouble with
medication, drugs, or alcohol, has expressed suicidal thoughts, or may be the victim of foul play.

Each student is required via the emergency notification information form to identify the name and
telephone number of the individual(s), a primary and secondary contact, to be notified in case of
emergency or in the event that the student is reported missing. In the event the student is under the
age of 18 and is not emancipated, the College is required to have the primary emergency contact be a
custodial parent or guardian.
