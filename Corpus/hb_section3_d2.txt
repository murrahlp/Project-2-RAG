D. Campus Programming/Co-Curricular Policies

1. Eligibility To Participate In Co-Curricular Activities Policy
Students are eligible to participate in any co-curricular activity if they are enrolled at Beloit
College, taking at least three units of credit (twelve semester hours) per semester, and
making satisfactory progress toward a degree. Normally, students are expected to
complete degree requirements in eight semesters, but may do so in nine semesters.

Students on academic probation are not restricted from participation except as prohibited
by the regulations of specific organizations and/or their degree of probation as specified by
the Academic Performance Committee. This definition of eligibility conforms to the Beloit
College philosophy, which regards athletics and other co-curricular activities as
opportunities that should be made available to all of our enrolled full-time students.

2. Clubs and Organizations
Funds Policy
Each student pays a student activities fee each semester. The money is collected by the
College and is dispersed by the Beloit Student Government (BSG). The money is allocated
by BSG and will be placed in the account of each recognized club or organization in August
for the fall term and in January for spring semester. It is against College policy to request or
spend student activities fee monies through the use of deception, false information, or for
purposes other than those stated and agreed upon in the approved funding request or
proposal.

Students and organizations that violate this policy will be subject to disciplinary action. Sanctions
organizations may receive include probation, freezing of club accounts, being temporarily placed
in an inactive or dissolved status as a club or organization, community service or other sanctions
as determined by the Office of Student Engagement and Leadership (SEAL).

Each organization will be required to furnish names of their club’s advisor, club leaders, and one
individual who will have club approval to access the club account each semester (herein, “the
financial person”). SEAL will only authorize club financial transactions and reimbursements for
the financial person or for others with this person’s approval.

Individuals who use club funds for personal use (embezzlement) or without proper authorization
will be subject to the Judicial Hearing process and, if found responsible, are eligible for the full
range of sanctions as listed.

Contracting Policy
Any student/student group wishing to hire a performer, entertainer, or non-students to
perform a service on campus (herein referred to as “artist”), must have a written contract

made for the services performed and signed ONLY by the SEAL Director or the Director of
Residential Life. Students may not enter into contractual agreements on behalf of the
College or their club. (C-Haus contracting is coordinated through the C-Haus Manager and
the SEAL Director).

Contracts can only be made if money for the entire amount of the service has been approved
by the funding board or a club’s budget. Contracts are never to be signed if the money is not
appropriated.

Payment: A Beloit College check must pay all services performed by artists. No other forms of
payment are permissible. A contract and W-9/Vendor form need to be signed by the artist and
turned into the Student Engagement and Leadership Office at least one week before the
performance. No exceptions will be made. Failure to meet this deadline may result in delayed
payment. Artists are paid by check in the week following the event.

Terms of contracting: Any student wishing to make offers to artists must first meet with the
Director of Student Engagement and Leadership (SEAL) for a formal training session. No
contracts will be authorized for students who do not follow through on this mandatory
training session.

Verbal Contracts: Verbally committing to any artist, performance dates, or similar items can
result in the student personally paying for their commitment to the artist. Beloit College will not
tolerate or support verbal contracts. Artists can be contacted for price quotes but under no
circumstances should a student agree to anything until a meeting is made with the SEAL Director
to go over specifics. Additionally, no artists outside of or within the College are to be contracted
to perform in violation of the quiet hours (and locations) of the College, particularly during exam
weeks and study days. Failure to abide by the above policies may result in disciplinary action.

All performances and services paid by student activities monies must be contracted. All other
performances and events are strongly encouraged to be contracted to protect all parties
involved.

Fundraising Policy
Beloit has a full-time professional staff of fundraisers and alumni specialists to support the needs
of the College. Students may not raise money or ask for gifts-in-kind without first consulting with
the Office of Advancement. Advancement staff is available to provide guidance and support to
help students in achieving their fundraising goals. Contact the Office of Advancement
(advancement@beloit.edu) to discuss:

● Gifts of any type from alumni or parents.
● Soliciting funds or gifts-in-kind from any local business (including goods, services, cash, or gift
cards).

● Fundraising plans, event ideas, or other fundraising activities requiring coordination with the
College or external donors.
● Questions about fundraising policies or best practices.

Students should consult the Office of Advancement before beginning any fundraising activity.
Early coordination helps ensure fundraising efforts align with College policies, avoid conflicting
requests to donors or community partners, and maximize the success of fundraising initiatives.

Program Activity & Event Planning
If you are thinking of planning an event, please contact the Director of Student Engagement
and Leadership (SEAL). Go to the SEAL website
(https://www.beloit.edu/offices/student-engagement-leadership/resources/) for guidance
on event planning.

Events requiring Facilities requests for any outdoor spaces near the residence halls (such as
the Chapin Quad or Aldrich Field) require the approval of the Director of Residential Life.
Events requiring Facilities requests for any outdoor spaces on the academic end of campus
(such as the Pearsons Quad) require the approval of the Dean of Students Office. Students
planning outdoor events close to the Mounds on campus must include provisions to
prevent anyone from disturbing the Mounds. Requests for Facilities set up must be
requested by the SEAL Director.

Program Presentation
Free inquiry and free expression are essential attributes of the community of scholars.
Therefore, recognized student groups are allowed to invite and to present any speaker,
performer, or dramatic or musical group of their own choosing, provided they follow
regular procedures for scheduling facilities and placing the event on the calendar. The
appearance of such guests implies neither approval nor objection to the views expressed
by either the students or other members of the College. Unless sponsored by a recognized
campus group adhering to the above-mentioned procedures, outside speakers,
demonstrators, and performers are prohibited from using College property to convey their
message.

In addition, sponsors must adhere to the following policies:
1. Even if no financial obligation is incurred for an event, a sponsor is required to schedule
the event on the Master Calendar before booking the speaker or performer.
2. Sponsors of events should be prepared to verify availability of necessary funds
before an event is booked. A College account number is usually required to cover
any expenses.
3. All publicity distributed beyond campus, including news releases, posters, mailers,
and flyers must be channeled through the Office of Communications and Marketing
(C&M).

4. No solicitations for funding from off-campus individuals, businesses, or groups
should be undertaken without first receiving the approval of the Office of
Advancement.
5. All political activity must be approved by the Director of C&M prior to publicity
notices.
6. All purchasing of off-campus printing services, including typesetting and printing,
should be done with the assistance of C&M.

Please Note: Consistent with its educational mission, the College encourages campus groups to invite
speakers with a wide range of perspectives. Speakers on campus must not only be protected from violence
and harassment, but also be given an opportunity to be heard. Community members and visitors may not
suppress others’ speech by obstructing, shouting down, or otherwise preventing them from being heard.

3. Publicity, Solicitation, Signs

Chalking Policy
The use of water-soluble chalk is permitted on horizontal surfaces exposed to weather. Areas
where chalking is not permitted include buildings, vertical surfaces (walls), painted surfaces,
porches, wooden surfaces, in the MI tunnels, under the walkways of the ’64 Halls, and other
surfaces not exposed to weather. Chalking is not permitted on the steps, ramps or red brick area
of Middle College. The use of spray-on chalk or any other formulation (including the mixing of
chalk and water as a paint) is prohibited on the Beloit College campus and sidewalks.

Chalking is also subject to the policies below under Signs/Posters/Digital Signage including the
“BTYB”, “TDD” and not promoting alcohol or drug use. Those responsible for chalking in
inappropriate areas may be charged for the cost to clean or repaint the surface and other
disciplinary action may result. The take down date (“TDD”) for any chalking may not be longer
than two (2) weeks from the date of the initial chalking.

Commercial Solicitation Policy
As a general rule, the College prohibits the use of campus grounds, buildings or vehicles
parked on campus by College or non-College personnel (including students) for the purpose
of commercial solicitation of students or sales of any type. All individuals or organizations
seeking exceptions must apply for the appropriate permit at the Dean of Students or, if
residence halls are concerned, the Director of Residential Life.

Signs/Posters/Digital Signage
Campus signs should promote educational engagement or serve as a means to advertise
campus events. All signs should respect other individuals and the community as a whole.
Note that political signs are also subject to the Political Sign Policy. (This policy is for paper
posters, see chalking policy for outdoor chalk advertising.)
