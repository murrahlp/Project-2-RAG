SECTION I: General Behavior Expectations
A. Stay Informed: Read and Understand All Rules, Policies, and
Standards
By enrolling, students are held responsible for reading, understanding, and following all rules, policies,
standards, and expectations expressed within the Student Handbook and all other campus publications.

B. Follow the Laws: Civil/Criminal US or International Laws
Students accept full responsibility for their own actions under federal, state and local laws (U.S. or
abroad). The College provides no immunity from the consequences of illegal acts and the College
does not provide legal counsel for its students. The College or the aggrieved party always has
recourse to the civil authorities (U.S. or abroad) for violations of civil/criminal law. Ignorance of
laws does not mitigate the responsibility for following the laws.

C. On-Campus Behavior
A Beloit College student is expected to conduct themselves in a manner that respects themselves,
other students, and other members of the College community. A student may not behave in a
manner that denies or interferes with another member’s expression of convictions, right to
academic freedom, or the performance of the duties or functions of others.

D. Prohibited Behavior
The following acts are prohibited. Individuals violating these student regulations and policies may
face disciplinary action as outlined in the Adjudication of Violations of Expectations, Policies or
Standards section. Those that are also crimes may also face criminal charges. Many of these
prohibited behaviors have separate policies which describe them more specifically later in this
Student Handbook. This is a general overview:

● Violence, of any type, that is harmful to another member of the College community or any
property of an individual, the College or the community.
● Assault, which includes forced or coerced sexual contact, or the threat thereof against any
member or guest of the College. (See Policy Against Sexual Harassment and Associated
Grievance Procedures).
● Discrimination or harassment based on race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy, sexual
orientation, gender identity, and transgender status), national origin, age (40 or older),
disability, genetic information, protected veteran status, or any other characteristic protected
by applicable federal, state, or local law.
● Sexual, personal, physical, emotional or psychological harassment, or the threat thereof,
including hazing, against any member or guest of the College.
● Phone or social media harassment, which includes prank calls; threatening, obscene or
otherwise harassing use of a phone or any social media platform.
