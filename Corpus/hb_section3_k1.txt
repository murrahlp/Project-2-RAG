1. Emergency Notifications & Community Alerts (Timely Warnings)

The Safety and Security Office is the primary office which sends “Rave Alerts,” also known as “Emergency
Notifications,” via the Rave Alert mass notification system. We utilize email, text, and phone calls to alert
our campus about an active and immediate threat to the life safety of campus. The College uses the
CLEAR framework when determining whether an Emergency Notification should be issued.

● C – Confirmed
● L - Life-threatening
● E - Emergency
● A - Active (or Imminent)
● R - Reportable Geography (on our campus or public property directly adjacent to it) at large.

The Safety and Security Director, Emergency Operations Center Manager, Dean of Students, Human
Resources Director, and other Senior Leadership may be involved with the decision to send a Rave Alert if
there is ample time to consider whether the threat is truly CLEAR. We will only send out an “ALL-CLEAR”
alert if we already sent a Rave Alert, and only once it is reasonably safe to resume normal activities.

The College also issues Community Alerts, also known as Timely Warnings, through email
communications using the [Safety] tag. These notifications are intended to inform the College community
about Clery-reportable crimes or other safety concerns that represent an ongoing or continuing threat
within the College’s Clery Geography, even when the threat is not considered an immediate or imminent
danger to life.

Not all crimes or hazardous incidents will result in a Timely Warning. Timely Warnings are issued when
they are determined to be appropriate and likely to assist in protecting the campus community or
preventing similar incidents. Decisions regarding whether to issue a Timely Warning are made on a
case-by-case basis and typically involve the Director of Safety and Security in consultation with
appropriate College officials, such as the Dean of Students or Human Resources Director.
