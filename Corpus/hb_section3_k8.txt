7. Severe Weather/Tornado Plans
At various times during the year, we may have a severe weather alert and/or a tornado watch/warning
(especially in the spring months of March, April, and May). It is important that all students be aware of
any severe weather conditions. These warnings are only sent after a tornado or destructive storm has
been confirmed, and the NWS has issued an alert for the City of Beloit. Rock County will sound their
tornado sirens for a three- to five-minute period to alert all residents of the County, whether or not the
warning affects the City of Beloit.

Security will send out Rave Alerts via email, text message, and phone call if a Tornado Warning affects
the City of Beloit, and will only send out the All-Clear message after the National Weather Service (NWS)
alert has expired. Security will use their best judgement on whether to send out an alert for the Severe
Thunderstorm Warning or other severe weather. If they do, they will send out an All-Clear message after
the NWS has expired, or the severe weather has passed. Rock County will not sound an all-clear siren or
message.

All students are to follow the directions sent via Rave Alerts. For severe weather, students are to move to
an area of safety typically the lowest level in an interior room or hallway of the nearest building, away
from windows and doors. You should remain in a place of safety until the NWS alert expires, or you
receive an All-Clear text from the college.

You can view any NWS alerts which may affect the City of Beloit here:
https://forecast.weather.gov/product.php?site=NWS&issuedby=MKX&product=HWO.

Rock County Outdoor Warning Siren System Testing Information:
● Occurs on the First Wednesday of each month at 12:05 PM, April thru October.
● Steady tone of only 2 minutes
● Sirens are not tested when inclement weather is being experienced within the County.

8. Emergency Phones
Emergency phones are located across campus to allow ready access to Security and other services in the
event of an emergency. Phones include blue-light towers and telephone boxes attached to the walls of
buildings. The most up to date Emergency Phone locations and maps can be found online at:
https://www.beloit.edu/offices/security/emergency-numbers-phones/

Press the red round “Emergency” button on an emergency phone and it will automatically call Security.
You may also dial any four-digit campus extension you wish to reach.

Students are encouraged to use these for issues where they might not have their phone with them, like
lockouts in severe weather, or to notify Security about a non-emergency.

Please take a moment to familiarize yourself with the locations of the emergency phones around campus
on the map and list below.
