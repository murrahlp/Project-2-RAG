3. Publicity, Solicitation, Signs

Chalking Policy
The use of water-soluble chalk is permitted on horizontal surfaces exposed to weather. Areas
where chalking is not permitted include buildings, vertical surfaces (walls), painted surfaces,
porches, wooden surfaces, in the MI tunnels, under the walkways of the ’64 Halls, and other
surfaces not exposed to weather. Chalking is not permitted on the steps, ramps or red brick area
of Middle College. The use of spray-on chalk or any other formulation (including the mixing of
chalk and water as a paint) is prohibited on the Beloit College campus and sidewalks.

Chalking is also subject to the policies below under Signs/Posters/Digital Signage including the
“BTYB”, “TDD” and not promoting alcohol or drug use. Those responsible for chalking in
inappropriate areas may be charged for the cost to clean or repaint the surface and other
disciplinary action may result. The take down date (“TDD”) for any chalking may not be longer
than two (2) weeks from the date of the initial chalking.

Commercial Solicitation Policy
As a general rule, the College prohibits the use of campus grounds, buildings or vehicles
parked on campus by College or non-College personnel (including students) for the purpose
of commercial solicitation of students or sales of any type. All individuals or organizations
seeking exceptions must apply for the appropriate permit at the Dean of Students or, if
residence halls are concerned, the Director of Residential Life.

Signs/Posters/Digital Signage
Campus signs should promote educational engagement or serve as a means to advertise
campus events. All signs should respect other individuals and the community as a whole.
Note that political signs are also subject to the Political Sign Policy. (This policy is for paper
posters, see chalking policy for outdoor chalk advertising.)

Sign Content:
● Signs cannot promote alcohol or drug use.
● Due to copyright laws, no videos/movies designed for private use may be advertised.
(This includes movies/videos rented, owned, or streamed without explicit permission to
share for any event.)
● Signs must cite sources.
● All signs are required to include “Brought to You By” (BTYB:). The BTYB must
provide a legitimate primary contact (individual student, Beloit-recognized
organization, or campus department) affiliated with the sign.
○ The BTYB person/organization is responsible for promptly removing your
posters after the event advertised occurs, or if the posters are rendered
illegible.
○ The BTYB person/organization is required to put the TDD (Take Down Date) on
the sign.
● Students are expected to respect signs and the person/organization who put them up.
Don’t:
○ Tear signs down for current events
○ Intentionally cover up posters for current events
○ Deface posters with drawings, writings, false statements, etc.

Inquiries should be directed to the Director of Student Engagement and Leadership.

Sign Placement:
Signs are not to be placed on glass doors, glass entryways or the glass portion of any door,
any painted surfaces (unless using blue painter’s tape), nor any metal, sidewalks, fire exits
or alarms, railings, lamp posts, or garbage cans. Students are not to use duct tape, masking
tape, or packing tape. Tacks are only to be used on appropriate bulletin boards. However, if
determined necessary for public health and safety, members of Beloit College Operations
(Security, Housekeeping, Student Life or Facilities staff) may place signs as needed).

4. Social Host Policy
All social functions/gatherings occurring in the living areas of campus must be sponsored by or
hosted by students who take full responsibility for the function.

1. The host(s) is responsible for monitoring the behavior of those attending, ensuring
College policies are followed and ensuring clean-up activities are completed.
2. The host(s) will work with the Residence Life Coordinator and residents to determine if
a social gathering is acceptable prior to the event.
3. The host(s) will ensure there are students checking IDs at the entrance. Only Beloit
College students and their registered guests are to be admitted.
4. The host(s) assumes all costs associated with the function. This includes, but is not
limited to, cleaning, damages, repairs, etc.
5. The host(s) will call Security and/or the RA, or Organization’s President/Lead position, if
a gathering is out of control.
6. The host(s) will monitor the area for noise problems and immediately respond to noise
complaints.
7. The host(s) is responsible for the behavior of all guests and agrees to be present for the
entire event.
8. For gatherings that are planned in advance, or advertised (either in writing, via social media,
or by word of mouth) a list of those responsible must be provided to the Residence Life
Office no later than 48 hours prior to the event. This list shall include the person responsible
and serving as host and a list of each person who is checking ID’s and monitoring the event.
