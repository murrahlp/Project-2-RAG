#  Four Answerable
## 1. What weapons are prohibited on campus?
   
A. Prohibited weapons include but are not limited to: firearms (including those carried under a concealed
carry permit or license), pellet or BB guns, ammunition, knives (other than small utility tools), explosives,
fireworks, chemicals intended to cause harm, laser pointers, swords, axes, paintball guns, and any object
used or intended to be used in a threatening or dangerous manner. (Handbook)

## 2. Can I drink in any academic buildings ?
   
A. Alcoholic beverages may not be served or consumed in academic areas unless prior approval is
received from the Department/Division Chair or Provost. (Handbook)

## 3. Can I use spraychalk on beloit campus/sidewalks?
   
A. The use of spray-on chalk or any other formulation (including the mixing of
chalk and water as a paint) is prohibited on the Beloit College campus and sidewalks. (Handbook)

## 4. What is the lock out policy on campus ?
   
A. Residents are expected to carry room and building keys (and IDs) with them at all
times. Locked doors and attention to keys are essential to students’ personal safety and the safety of
their property. In the event a student is locked out of their room during a college work day, the student
should call the Residence Life Office (608-363-2350). If it is after office hours or on the weekend, the
student should contact Security, who can let them in. If the student loses their key, they should contact
Residence Life immediately, fill out a key replacement form, and take it to Security to have a new key cut
for their room. (Handbook)

## 7. Where can I get my Beloit College transcript ?

A. Students may request transcripts
of their permanent academic records at any time; however, transcripts will not be released without the
student’s authorizing signature and approval from the Accounting Office. Requests for transcripts may be
submitted online using the instructions found at www.beloit.edu/ofces/ registrar/transcripts-diplomasverifcation-letters/. A fee is charged for each transcript issued or Beloit College transcripts are available through the National Student Clearinghouse (NSC). (Course Catalog and Handbook)
